# Abstract: novathesis-ulisboa-fcul-v8.0.0

This is a customized LaTeX template for theses and reports at **Faculdade de Ciências da Universidade de Lisboa (FCUL)**, following the most recent [FCUL guidelines for PhD (in Portuguese)](https://ciencias.ulisboa.pt/ensino/estudantes-atuais/informacoes-academicas/avaliacao-de-conhecimentos-e-certificacao/admissao-a-provas-academicas/3-ciclo) and [FCUL guidelines for MSc (in Portuguese)](https://ciencias.ulisboa.pt/ensino/estudantes-atuais/informacoes-academicas/avaliacao-de-conhecimentos-e-certificacao/admissao-a-provas-academicas/2-ciclo).

This template is a streamlined and fine-tuned version of the robust **NOVAthesis framework (v8.0.0)**, trimmed down and optimized to provide FCUL students with a streamlined experience, meeting FCUL's academic formatting standards without the complexity of the original multi-institution template. 

### Key Features:

* **FCUL-Ready:** Pre-configured with the correct margins, cover page styles, and fonts required by FCUL.
* **Streamlined:** Unnecessary files and configurations for other universities and languages (other than PT and EN) have been removed, making the template lighter and easier to navigate.
* **Modern Academic Standards:**
    * **AI Disclosure:** Integrated support for the new Artificial Intelligence Disclosure Statement.
    * **SDGs:** Visual enumeration of the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) for modern research impact reporting.
* **User-Friendly Design:** Optimized for both LaTeX beginners and power users, ensuring a clean and efficient workflow.
